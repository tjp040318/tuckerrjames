Privacy policy

tuckerrjames-social · Effective October 19, 2026

tuckerrjames-social ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish his own videos to his own YouTube channel. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

Information the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app asks for two YouTube permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to post videos and Shorts the owner has approved to his channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's name and ID, so the app can confirm it is posting to the right channel.

The app does not access anyone else's account, and it does not collect personal information, contacts, comments, viewing history or any data about viewers.

How the information is used and stored

  • The channel name and ID are used only to check that uploads go to the owner's own channel.
  • The sign-in credentials (an OAuth token) are kept as encrypted secrets in a private repository and are used only to make the uploads described above.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it stops the app from uploading anything; the stored token then no longer works and is deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
