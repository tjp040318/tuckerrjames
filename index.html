tuckerrjames-social

A private publishing tool for the tuckerrjames travel channel

tuckerrjames-social is a small personal tool that its owner uses to post his own travel videos and short clips to his own YouTube channel, tuckerrjames. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it does

  • Uploads videos and Shorts that the owner has reviewed and approved to his own YouTube channel.
  • Reads the name and ID of the signed-in channel, to make sure uploads only ever go to the right channel.

It uses the YouTube Data API. It does not read, collect or store anything about other people.
